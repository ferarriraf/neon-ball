Privacy Policy

Neon Escape — last updated 9 August 2026

Neon Escape is a personal automation tool used exclusively by its owner.

  • It does not collect, store, or share any personal data from third parties.
  • The only data processed are: music files (MIDI or audio) that the owner places on their own server, and the owner's own TikTok OAuth tokens, which are stored locally on that private server and never shared with anyone.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no advertising, no database of users.
  • TikTok data is accessed only through the official TikTok for Developers APIs, limited to the scopes granted by the owner (user.info.basic to confirm which account is connected, and video.publish to post videos), and used solely to publish the owner's own generated videos on the owner's own account.
  • No data is transmitted to any third party. Notifications about the tool's activity are sent only to a private channel owned by the operator.
  • The owner may revoke the app's access at any time from their TikTok account settings, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

For any question, contact the owner through GitHub.
