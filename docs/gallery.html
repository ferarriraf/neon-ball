What a video looks like

Three moments from a run, drawn here with the same shapes and colours the renderer uses. Each sphere picks its own neon hue, and the rings of a sphere drift across a small hue range from the inside out.

The play area

The ball, its trail, the rings left to clear, a counter and the spectrum analyser reacting to the soundtrack.

A ring gives way

The cleared layer dilates, its opening yawns, and it evaporates into motes. A chime marks the moment — the melody itself never skips a note.

Sphere completed

Every ring cleared: a shockwave spreads, sparks fly and a fanfare plays. Then a fresh sphere appears in a new colour — or the video ends.

Two videos a day

One clip goes out in the late morning and one in the late afternoon, each at a randomised minute inside its window so the account never posts on a metronome. The description rotates through a short list, so two consecutive videos never carry the same caption.
