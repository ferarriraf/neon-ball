# Privacy Policy

**bot-scrap** is a personal automation tool used exclusively by its owner.

- It does not collect, store, or share any personal data from third parties.
- The only data processed are: the owner's own Instagram posts (videos and captions,
  publicly available) and the owner's own TikTok OAuth tokens, which are stored locally
  on the owner's private server and never shared with anyone.
- No analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no database of users.
- TikTok data is accessed only through the official TikTok for Developers APIs, limited
  to the scopes granted by the owner (user.info.basic and video publishing), and used
  solely to publish the owner's own videos on the owner's own account.

For any question, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
